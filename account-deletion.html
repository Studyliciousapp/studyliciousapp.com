Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your Studylicious account and associated data at any time, in two ways:

Option 1 — In the app

Go to Profile > Settings > Delete Account and confirm. This immediately deletes your account and data.

Option 2 — By email

Send an email to info@studyliciousapp.com from your registered university email address, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". This option is available even if you don't have the app installed.

What gets deleted

When you request account deletion, we permanently delete:

  • Your account profile (name, email, university, profile photo)
  • Your booking history
  • Your subscription status and related records

Timeline

We process account deletion requests within 30 days of receiving your request.

Retained data

Some data may be retained for a limited period where required for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes (for example, records of subscription payments), consistent with our Privacy Policy.
