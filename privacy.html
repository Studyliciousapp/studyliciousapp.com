Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-06-09

This privacy policy applies to the Studylicious app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Studylicious (collectively, the "Application"). Studylicious is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it, including:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address
  • Pages visited, time and date of visit, time spent on pages
  • Time spent on the Application
  • Your mobile operating system

The Service Provider may require personally identifiable information including: email, name, phone number, date of birth, gender, profile photo, university.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Application or its third-party SDKs may use cookies, SDKs, pixels, and similar technologies to support functionality, analytics, or service delivery.

Location Information

The Application collects your device's location to provide location-based features such as displaying nearby restaurants and bars on an interactive map.

Data Storage and Cloud Services

The Application uses Google Cloud Firestore (Google Ireland Limited, region: europe-west1) to store user data including account information, bookings, deal interactions, and subscription status. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase's data processing terms.

Subscription and Payment Processing

Subscriptions are processed through RevenueCat and Apple's App Store. Payment information is handled entirely by Apple and RevenueCat and is not stored by the Service Provider.
