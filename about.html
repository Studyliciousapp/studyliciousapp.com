Why I built Studylicious

Luca Ecker, founder

A few months ago, this was just an idea.

I was a student here in Leuven myself, and going out isn't cheap — especially when you don't have a lot of money to spare. I kept wondering why there wasn't an easy way to find real, worthwhile deals at the places I actually wanted to go.

So I built Studylicious: an app made only for students, where you can find real deals at restaurants, cafés and bars around Leuven, and actually save money doing the things you'd do anyway.

Every deal in the app is real, and every partner is a real spot in this city. New students get 3 months completely free, automatically, the moment they register with their student email.

If you're a student in Leuven, I hope this saves you a bit of money on your next coffee, lunch, or night out.

— Luca
